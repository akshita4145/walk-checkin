# Scope
Person: A student walking home alone from the library after midnight.
Moment: Friends assume she got home; group-chat check-ins fail if she forgets or her phone dies.
Core feature: She starts a walk with an expected arrival time. If she doesn't tap "I'm home"
  by then, her friend's page turns to OVERDUE, even if her phone is dead.
Demo moment: Start a 1-minute walk, open the friend link on a second screen, wait, and the
  status flips to red "Overdue." Tap "I'm home" and it turns green.
Not building: accounts/login, SMS or push notifications, live GPS, maps, a contacts list, a mobile app.
